# Marvel Rivals Chronicle

**A spoiler-free chronological reading guide to the lore of Marvel Rivals.**

Marvel Rivals tells its story across Hero Stories, Gallery Cards, limited-time events, collectables, comics, maps, videos, and other sources. Many of these pieces are released at different times or become unavailable in-game, making it difficult to know what to read — and especially **when to read it without spoiling later events**.

**Marvel Rivals Chronicle** organizes that material into a single recommended narrative reading order.

> **Current release: v1.1 — Updated through Season 9.5**
> **204 lore entries**

## Reading Philosophy

The goal of Marvel Rivals Chronicle is not to retell the game's story.

It is designed to answer a simpler question:

**“What should I read next?”**

The Chronicle deliberately avoids revealing major events, identities, deaths, betrayals, resolutions, or other narrative surprises whenever possible.

This means it can be used alongside the game by someone experiencing the lore for the first time.

---

## What Counts as an Entry?

Marvel Rivals spreads narrative information across several different formats.

The Chronicle currently tracks material such as:

| Code | Type                          |
| ---- | ----------------------------- |
| `HS` | Hero Story                    |
| `GC` | Gallery Card                  |
| `EV` | Narrative Event               |
| `IT` | In-game Item / Collectable    |
| `IC` | Infinity Comic / Tie-in Comic |

Not every cosmetic item, mission, challenge, or event receives an individual Chronicle entry.

Material is included when it contains meaningful story information or helps establish the chronology.

---

## Permanent MRC IDs

Every Chronicle entry receives a permanent ID such as:

`MRC-0045`

These IDs are intentionally separate from the reading-order number.

If new lore is released later and needs to be inserted between older stories, the **reading order may change, but the MRC ID does not**.

For example, an older entry may move from position 190 to position 193 after a new story is inserted before it, but its MRC ID will remain unchanged.

This allows external references, tools, discussions, and future versions of the Chronicle to remain compatible.

## Sources and Editorial Method

The Chronicle is assembled by comparing multiple sources rather than treating any single chronology as definitive.

Sources include:

* Official Marvel Rivals material
* Hero Stories
* Gallery entries
* In-game Collectables
* Limited-time narrative events
* Official Marvel Rivals news and season information
* Marvel Rivals Wiki archives
* Tie-in comics
* Existing community chronology research

When the chronological placement of an entry cannot be established with sufficient confidence, it may be marked as **Provisional** until additional material provides better evidence.

The Chronicle links to sources instead of reproducing complete copyrighted stories.

---

## Availability of Older Lore

Some Marvel Rivals story material was originally released through limited-time events or Gallery unlocks and may no longer be obtainable by new players.

The Chronicle identifies these cases whenever possible.

If an official source is unavailable, the guide links to the best reliable archival or reference source currently known.

Broken or improved source links are very welcome as contributions.

## AI-Assisted Development

For transparency, **Marvel Rivals Chronicle was created with extensive AI assistance**.

AI tools were used during parts of the research organization, editing, translation, data processing, formatting, document generation, and quality-control workflow.

The project was personally directed and reviewed by its creator, including editorial decisions, chronology placement, corrections, source checking, and approval of the final releases.

The final Chronicle is therefore an **AI-assisted fan project**, not an automatically generated or unreviewed lore database.
